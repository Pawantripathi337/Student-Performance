How the system works

1
Learn from data

Supervised learning: models learn the relationship between student features (marks, absences, study time…) and the known final grade.

2
Compare models

Linear Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest are trained and scored with MAE, MSE, RMSE and R². The best one is selected automatically.

3
Explain the result

The score is mapped to a category and a rule-based risk level, with plain-English reasons and recommendations.
